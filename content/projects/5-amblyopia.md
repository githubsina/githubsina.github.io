## short
EN: Each eye gets its own flicker in VR, and EEG shows which one wins.
中: 在 VR 里给每只眼睛各自的闪烁，用脑电看出哪只眼睛占上风。

## lede
EN: When the two eyes see different patterns, they compete. We used a VR headset as two separate screens and read the brain’s answer with SSVEP to measure that rivalry.
中: 两只眼睛看到不同的图案时，会相互竞争。我们把 VR 头显当作两块独立的屏幕，用 SSVEP 读取大脑的“答案”，来测量这种双眼竞争。

## caption
EN: The system: each eye gets its own flicker frequency (7 Hz and 12.5 Hz) inside the headset, while EEG picks up which eye wins.
中: 系统示意：头显里每只眼睛有各自的闪烁频率（7 Hz 和 12.5 Hz），脑电记录哪只眼睛胜出。

## story.1
EN: the problem | Glasses and stereoscopes struggle to show each eye its own image, or to change it dynamically, so binocular rivalry is hard to measure.
中: 问题 | 眼镜和立体镜很难给每只眼睛单独显示图像，也难以动态变化，所以双眼竞争很难测量。

## story.2
EN: what we built | SSVEP stimuli built into virtual scenes (trees, a lawn, a river) and shown separately to each eye, with live EEG processing and a rehabilitation game on top.
中: 我们做了什么 | 把 SSVEP 刺激嵌进虚拟场景（树、草地、河流），分别呈现给两只眼睛，实时处理脑电，并在此基础上做了一个康复小游戏。
