## story.1
EN: the problem | Automated equipment leaves little hands-on engagement, and students get buried in procedural steps, so the concepts teachers design for get lost.
中: 问题 | 自动化设备让学生动手的机会很少，学生又容易淹没在繁琐的步骤里，老师想传达的概念因此被忽略。

## story.2
EN: what I built | Co-designed with teachers and students: scan a QR code at a station and the matching module opens, with 3D molecules, step checklists, instant feedback and small rewards. Teachers edit it without Unity.
中: 我做了什么 | 与老师和学生共同设计：在工位扫一下二维码，对应模块就会打开，里面有 3D 分子、步骤清单、即时反馈和小奖励。老师不用 Unity 也能编辑。

## story.3
EN: what we learned | Students found it natural and fun, and liked learning at their own pace. MR’s extra questions pulled their attention back to the concepts behind each step.
中: 我们的发现 | 学生觉得它自然又有趣，喜欢按自己的节奏学习。MR 里额外的提问把他们的注意力拉回到每一步背后的概念上。
