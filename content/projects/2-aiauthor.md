## story.1
EN: the idea | Cleanrooms already have process flows, run cards and machine manuals. Instead of rebuilding them as MR content by hand, the tool reads them and drafts step-by-step training. The teacher reviews it in a web editor and publishes it to the headset in one go.
中: 构想 | 洁净室本来就有工艺流程、流程卡和设备手册。与其手工把它们重做成 MR 内容，不如让工具读取这些文档，起草分步培训。老师在网页编辑器里审核，一键发布到头显。

## story.2
EN: why it matters | It sits where AI meets engineering education: authoring cost is one of the main reasons MR stays in pilots. If AI handles the first draft and people keep the judgment, more courses can use hands-on MR training.
中: 为什么重要 | 这项工作位于 AI 与工程教育的交叉点：制作成本是 MR 一直停留在试点阶段的主要原因之一。如果 AI 负责初稿、由人来把关，就会有更多课程用上动手实践的 MR 培训。
