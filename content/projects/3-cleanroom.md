## story.1
EN: the problem | Training in semiconductor cleanrooms relies on one-to-one “master–apprentice” teaching that is hard to scale, and building MR content usually needs programming skills most educators don’t have.
中: 问题 | 半导体洁净室的培训依赖一对一的“师傅带徒弟”，难以规模化；而制作 MR 内容通常需要编程能力，大多数教育者并不具备。

## thread.1
EN: the workflow | Instructors build the training in a web editor, without code: steps, 3D models, videos, quizzes. Then they pin each step to the real equipment, in the office or on site, and students follow it on HoloLens 2.
中: 制作流程 | 老师在网页编辑器里零代码搭建培训：步骤、3D 模型、视频和小测验。再在办公室或现场把每一步锚定到真实设备上，学生戴上 HoloLens 2 跟着做。

## thread.2
EN: the invisible bubble | Students stopped imitating the teacher and worked on their own, but each one ended up inside a bubble of focus, talking less to peers. We suggest ways to step out of it when collaboration matters.
中: 看不见的气泡 | 学生不再模仿老师，而是独立操作；但每个人都进入了一个专注的“气泡”，和同伴的交流变少了。我们提出了在需要协作时走出气泡的办法。
