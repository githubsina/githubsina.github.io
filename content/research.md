## intro
EN: I study what happens to attention, cognitive load and skill when hands-on learning moves into mixed reality. I build the systems myself, then test them in real courses with controlled studies, questionnaires, eye-tracking and interaction logs, EEG and video coding, and turn the results into design principles. Supervised by Prof. Jürgen Brugger (EPFL) and Prof. Manu Kapur (ETH Zürich).
中: 我研究动手学习搬进混合现实之后，注意力、认知负荷和技能会发生什么变化。系统由我自己搭建，再放到真实课程中检验：对照实验、问卷、眼动与交互日志、脑电和视频编码，最后把结果凝练成设计原则。导师为 Jürgen Brugger 教授（EPFL）和 Manu Kapur 教授（ETH Zürich）。
